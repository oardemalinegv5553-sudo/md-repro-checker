Computational details

All simulations were performed with GROMACS 2022.3. The polymer chains were
described by the GAFF2 force field with partial charges derived from AM1-BCC.
Initial configurations of the solvent-free polymer melt were generated with
Packmol and relaxed through a protocol consisting of energy minimisation,
500 ps of annealing under NVT, and 5 ns of NPT equilibration. Production runs
were carried out in the NPT ensemble at 300 K and 1 bar using the
Parrinello-Rahman barostat and the Berendsen thermostat. A time step of 2 fs
was used, with bonds involving hydrogen constrained by the LINCS algorithm.
Electrostatic interactions were treated using the particle-mesh Ewald method.
The thermal conductivity was obtained from direct non-equilibrium molecular
dynamics: the simulation cell was divided into slabs along the z axis, and a
constant heat flux was imposed by velocity rescaling in the hot and cold slabs
held at 350 K and 250 K, respectively. Each production run lasted 20 ns.
